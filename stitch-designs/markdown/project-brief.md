# Project Brief: Maxfoot Website Redesign

## 1. Project Overview
The goal of this project is to redesign the digital presence for **Maxfoot Electric Bikes** (maxfoot.bike), drawing inspiration from the **Shopify Horizon** aesthetic. The new design aims to elevate the brand to a premium, high-performance position while maintaining its rugged, adventurous core.

## 2. Design Strategy: "High-Performance Adventure"
The visual identity centers on a balance between technical precision and outdoor exploration.

### Visual Principles
*   **Typography:** Using **Space Grotesk** to convey a modern, technical, and slightly industrial feel. Bold, uppercase headers are used for high-impact messaging.
*   **Color Palette:** A clean base of **Zinc Grays and Whites** allows the brand's signature **Amber/Gold (#FFC426)** to pop, highlighting key actions and technical specs.
*   **Imagery:** High-contrast, action-oriented photography showing the bikes in their natural environment (trails, urban landscapes) alongside detailed engineering close-ups.
*   **Layout:** Spacious, grid-based layouts with clear hierarchy and minimal decorative elements, ensuring the product and technology remain the focus.

## 3. Key User Flows & Screens

### 1. Discovery & Brand Story (Homepage)
*   **Goal:** Introduce the brand's "Conquer Every Terrain" ethos.
*   **Key Elements:** Hero banner with video/high-impact imagery, featured categories (Mountain, Cruisers, Cargo), and a high-level performance overview.

### 2. Product Exploration (Listing & Detail)
*   **Product Listing (PLP):** A robust catalog with filtering by bike type, motor power, and price range.
*   **Product Detail (PDP):** A conversion-focused page featuring high-res galleries, core specs (Range, Speed, Peak Power), and a deep-dive "Technical Architecture" section.

### 3. Technical Credibility (Technology Page)
*   **Goal:** Establish Maxfoot as an engineering leader.
*   **Content:** Detailed breakdowns of the Brushless Hub Architecture, Intelligent BMS (Battery Management System), and Aerospace Grade Frame construction.

### 4. Post-Purchase Support (Support Hub)
*   **Goal:** Provide a seamless service experience.
*   **Content:** Quick access to assembly guides, manuals, warranty claims, and a streamlined contact form.

## 4. Technical Specifications
*   **Framework:** Built with a component-based architecture for consistency.
*   **Responsiveness:** Desktop-first design with a clear path for mobile optimization.
*   **Design Tokens:** Integrated within the "High-Performance Adventure" design system for rapid iteration and brand consistency.

## 5. Success Metrics
*   Improved user engagement through clear visual hierarchy.
*   Increased conversion rates via streamlined PDP and "Add to Cart" flows.
*   Reduced support inquiries through a comprehensive and searchable Support Hub.
